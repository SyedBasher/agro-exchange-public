FROM LISTING TO SETTLEMENT

A simpler transaction path

The first product keeps the market mechanism simple: discover prices, post supply or demand, match, verify, deliver and record settlement.

01

Discover

See source-market prices, buyer demand and delivered-cost estimates.

02

Post

Enter commodity, quantity, quality, location, timing and price expectations.

03

Match

Rank feasible counterparties by delivered economics, fit and reliability.

04

Verify & deliver

Record weight, quality, dispatch, delivery and payment status against one trade ID.

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

The transaction layer becomes a data layer.

Every verified offer, match, route and completed trade can improve the next pricing and sourcing decision. The long-run asset is not only the marketplace; it is the market information generated by using it.
